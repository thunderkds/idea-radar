Decision

Problem: Should the MCP server's unauthenticated GET /health expose the idea count, run count and last-run time, or return only {ok: true|false} and keep the counts behind a token?
Option B: unauthenticated /health returns exactly {ok:true} (200 after a SELECT 1 with a 2 s timeout) or exactly {ok:false} (503); no counts and no token-gated variant. Decided by the Supervisor after a 3-Advocate Debate. ⚠ decided for you — please review
All three Advocates agreed the counts should not be public and that the response must carry no error text. Advocates 1 and 2 would add the counts behind a bearer token on the same route; Advocate 3, told to favour the smallest safe thing, proposed no counts route at all. Both forms are undoable, so the rule picks the smaller change. The cited lines were opened first: apps/mcp/src/server.ts:82 and :84, railway.json:19, PRD.md:58, docs/deploy.md:47. This amends acceptance criterion 5 of the T034 guide.
